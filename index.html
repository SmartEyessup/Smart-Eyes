Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2026
App: Smart Eyes Pro
Platform: Apple App Store (iOS / iPadOS)

SmartEyes respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app processes, how it is used, and what stays on your device. SmartEyes is designed as a visual assistance tool for blind and low-vision users; it is not a replacement for a cane, guide dog, or professional safety advice.

Information Collection and Use

SmartEyes uses the device camera and microphone to provide visual assistance and voice-based interaction. Camera and microphone data are processed to deliver app features such as scene description, text reading, barcode lookup, navigation assistance, and face recognition (in Advanced Mode).

Some features send images or text to cloud AI services only when you request them (for example, when you ask the app to describe what is in front of you). Face matching for people you save is performed on your device and is not sent to cloud providers for identification.

Face Recognition (Advanced Mode, version 1.29+)

When you use face recognition in Advanced Mode (with an active Smart Eyes Pro subscription, trial, or eligible promotional access), the app may:

  • Detect faces in the camera image on your device.
  • Create a numeric face profile on your device when you ask to remember someone (for example, “Remember this person as [name]”).
  • Store the person’s name and face profile in encrypted local storage on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not store photographs of saved faces for this feature.
  • Compare new faces to people you saved and tell you when someone is recognized.

Recognition may be announced:

  • When you ask who is in front of you (for example, “Who is this?”).
  • After a scene description, if a known or unknown face is clearly visible (the app may offer to save an unknown person only in that situation).
  • Automatically during continuous description — while the app describes the scene on a timer you set in Settings, it may briefly announce a saved person’s name and direction (left, center, or right) before the next scene update, without you asking “who is this.”

Face data stays on your device. We do not upload saved face profiles or face embeddings to our servers. Face recognition is not available in Basic Mode.

You may remove a saved person by voice (for example, “Forget [name]”) or by uninstalling the app, which deletes locally stored app data including saved face profiles.

Speech Recognition

Speech input is used to understand your commands and questions. Audio may be processed on-device and/or through Apple’s speech recognition services, depending on your device and settings. We do not use your voice to build advertising profiles.

Location Information

Location data is used only when you activate the Emergency Assist feature. It is included in the emergency message you choose to send at that moment. SmartEyes does not track your location in the background for other features.

Contacts

If you select a contact for Emergency Assist, only the phone number you choose is stored locally on your device for that feature. The app does not upload your address book to our servers.

Subscriptions

Paid features are managed through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple processes payment; we do not receive your payment card details.

Data Sharing

SmartEyes does not sell your personal data. We do not share saved face profiles or face embeddings with third parties. Cloud AI providers may receive images or text only when you use features that require cloud processing, under their own terms and privacy practices.

Data Storage

SmartEyes does not operate a central user account database. The following may be stored locally on your device:

  • App preferences (language, speech rate, settings).
  • Emergency Assist phone number, if you set one.
  • Saved face profiles (name and numeric face data only), if you use face recognition in Advanced Mode — in encrypted storage where supported.

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data, including saved face profiles, subject to how iOS manages app data.

Children’s Privacy

SmartEyes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Network connections use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS) where applicable. Saved face profiles use encrypted storage on supported devices. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; use the app with appropriate caution for non–safety-critical assistance.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app functionality or legal requirements change. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
